The full RAGE engine, compiled to WebAssembly and running on WebGPU. Play it right here in your browser — or take it with you.

Play Instantly

Everything You Need To Know

Getting Started

  1. Open the browser build, or download and run the Windows installer.
  2. Wait while the engine streams its assets on the first load.
  3. Choose Story Mode or Sandbox Mode from the title screen.
  4. Click the game once to capture the mouse and begin playing.

Default Controls

  • MoveW A S D
  • LookMouse
  • SprintShift
  • InteractF
  • Aim / FireMouse
  • PauseEsc

Performance Tips

  • Use Chrome or Edge 133+ with hardware acceleration enabled.
  • Close other tabs and heavy apps before starting the game.
  • A first load may take a bit longer while data is cached.
  • Use a modern GPU and keep the browser in the foreground.

Frequently Asked

Why is the first load slow?
It streams the world assets on demand and stores them locally for quicker future runs.
Why does it need Chrome or Edge?
The game relies on WebGPU and modern WebAssembly support available in current Chromium browsers.
Does it work on Mac or Linux?
The browser version works on any machine with a recent supported browser and compatible GPU.
